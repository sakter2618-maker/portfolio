About me

The person behind the dashboards

I like questions that a spreadsheet can actually answer.

I am a data analyst and AI automation builder. My work starts with a business question, like "which products bring profit and which only bring volume?", and ends with a clear dashboard a manager can use in a meeting.

On the analytics side I clean and validate data with Python (pandas) and Excel, model and query it in SQL (PostgreSQL), apply statistics and machine learning (scikit-learn), and turn it into interactive dashboards with Streamlit and Plotly. Data analytics · Python · SQL

Retail & E-commerce Sales Analytics

An end-to-end analysis of 30 months of retail orders. Raw CSVs go in; a clean star-schema model, a five-page interactive Python dashboard and three business recommendations come out.

What I found
  • Orders fell 33% in 2026 while order value held steady, so the problem is traffic, not pricing
  • Deep discounts erase profit: margin drops from 31% to 1% above a 20% discount
  • 14.4% of potential sales leak through cancellations, returns and failed payments
What I did
  • Audited and cleaned the raw data in Python: 120 duplicates removed, missing values fixed, city names standardised
  • Loaded it into PostgreSQL and answered 12 business questions in SQL
  • Built a 5-page dashboard in Streamlit and Plotly with month, region, category and segment filters
  • Cross-checked every key number three ways (by hand, pandas and SQL) before reporting it

Finance · Risk · Machine learning

Banking & Insurance Risk Analytics

Loan default, customer risk segmentation, transaction anomaly detection and insurance-claim fraud signals, built for financial Data Analyst roles.

What I found
  • Unemployed (32%) and student (26%) borrowers default at 2–3× the 12.3% average
  • K-Means found a “Stressed Borrowers” segment with a 61% default rate
  • Claims over 2× the typical amount show 3.4× higher fraud; 4 red flags together mean 39% fraud
What I built
  • A logistic-regression default model (AUC 0.71) and a 0–100 risk score with Low to Critical tiers
  • Anomaly detection on 50,000 transactions: 7 rules plus Isolation Forest
  • 15 SQL queries, an Excel risk workbook and a live Streamlit app

Healthcare · Hospital operations

Healthcare Patient Analytics

Admissions, hospital performance, length of stay, treatment cost, 30-day readmissions and appointment no-shows across 5 hospitals, on synthetic, de-identified patient data.

What I found
  • One hospital readmits 20.8% of patients vs 13.5% at the best, and keeps them 1.1 days longer
  • Patients 60+ use 29.7% of all services and are readmitted 21.8% of the time
  • No-shows rise from 5.3% (same-day booking) to 21% when booked 30+ days ahead; SMS reminders cut them by 6 points
What I built
  • Python cleaning of a messy hospital export (22 logged fixes) and a PostgreSQL star schema with 18 queries
  • Readmissions derived with SQL window functions, matching Python exactly
  • An Excel cleaning workbook, a Power BI kit (DAX + theme) and a 7-page live dashboard
